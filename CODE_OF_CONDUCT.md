# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the work without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, and other project spaces. Do not post another person's location history or identifying case details, even as an example.

For a conduct concern, contact [the maintainer](https://github.com/IzaanAnwar) to arrange a private channel. Do not post sensitive incident details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or ban accounts. They will consider context and explain decisions to affected participants where practical.
